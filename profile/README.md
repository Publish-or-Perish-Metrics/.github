## What Is Publish or Perish

Publish or Perish is a Windows program for retrieving and analyzing citation metrics. A researcher checking papers they authored, or a committee member asked to evaluate a file, opens Publish or Perish citation metrics. Publish or Perish h-index is the familiar summary. Publish or Perish scholar lookup is the query against supported sources under the terms of those sources. Publish or Perish author metrics is the table you export into a report you write.

VOSviewer would map a corpus. CitNetExplorer would show lineages. This tool asks how often items are cited and how those counts summarize. It is only as honest as the index you queried and the name disambiguation you did. Homonyms are your problem to clean.

Query authors and papers you have a right to look up: yourself, a candidate who submitted a CV, a public academic profile. Do not automate harvesting of private or paywalled records beyond what your account and the source allow.

## Starting with Publish or Perish

Prerequisites: A PC you administer and an author or paper list you are allowed to evaluate.

1. **Prepare:** Write the author string as it appears on papers you authored or on the CV you were given.
2. **Configure:** Choose a supported source in Publish or Perish scholar lookup that your access allows.
3. **Test:** Run a short query and clean homonyms in Publish or Perish citation metrics.
4. **Refine:** Check Publish or Perish h-index against a known paper count you already verified.
5. **Apply:** Export Publish or Perish author metrics into the personnel or CV folder you keep.
6. **Maintain:** Rerun on a dated file, and do not treat one index as the whole story.
